# Rebuild the WCTC website

## What I’ll build
- Recreate WCTC as a polished six-page nonprofit website: Home, About, Services, Sponsorship, Ways to Give, and Contact.
- Keep the organization name, tagline, mission, history, service details, sponsorship process, and published contact information.
- Use the supplied nature and community photography throughout the site.
- Make navigation clear on desktop and mobile, with a compact mobile menu.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the original site’s nature-led identity while improving hierarchy, spacing, readability, and accessibility.
- Use a restrained forest green, warm paper, charcoal, and coral accent palette.
- Pair an editorial serif display face with a clean, approachable sans serif.
- Add subtle reveal and image movement while respecting reduced-motion settings.

## Page structure
- **Home:** immersive seedling image, mission statement, three service pillars, and fiscal sponsorship pathways.
- **About:** mission, story, and core values.
- **Services:** organized service categories and consultation call-to-action.
- **Sponsorship:** overview, three-step process, eligible project types, and exclusions.
- **Ways to Give:** donation pathways and a direct contact invitation; no payment processing is implied or added.
- **Contact:** email, phone, mailing address, and direct contact actions.

## Technical details
- Build each navigation item as its own shareable page with unique search and social metadata.
- Reuse a shared header, footer, page banner, and action components.
- Keep the site fully frontend-only; email and phone actions open the visitor’s existing apps.
- Verify all six pages at desktop and mobile sizes and resolve any build or runtime errors.
